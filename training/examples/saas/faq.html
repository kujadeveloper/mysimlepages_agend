FAQ

Are e-Fatura invoices legally valid?

Yes. We are connected to GİB through a licensed special integrator, so invoices are legally valid in Turkey.

Where is my data stored?

Turkish invoices are archived in Turkey as required by law; everything else is stored in the EU (Frankfurt).

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, with one click. You keep read access and can export all invoices as PDF and XML.

Do you handle EU reverse-charge VAT?

Yes. Add your client's VAT ID and the correct reverse-charge note is added automatically.
